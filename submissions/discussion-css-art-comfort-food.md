Two entries for the Comfort Food Edition. No SVG, no images, no canvas — every
shape in both is a `div`, a gradient, a `clip-path` or a shadow.

## 🌙 Midnight Fridge Raid

A dark kitchen at 2am, lit only by an open fridge door.

The brief says "or the dish you grew up eating" — so comfort food isn't a
recipe, it's a moment, and I drew the moment instead of the meal.

A feed full of ramen and pancakes is bright, warm and daylit; one dark scene
with a single hot light source stands out on contrast alone.

## ☕ Cutting Chai

A terracotta kulhad of masala chai, whole spices on the table, biscuit waiting
to be dunked.

Four o'clock, every day, for as long as anyone can remember — the comfort food
that isn't food.

Unglazed clay was the whole reason for picking it: matte is far kinder to
layered gradients than glass will ever be.
